## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Wiring Inside Walls
Real ghar mein wires ANDAR walls mein hidden hote hain. Yahi aaj hum karenge!

**Wire Management:**
- Wires walls ke andar ya along karo
- Color coding zaroori hai:
  - Red = Positive (+)
  - Black = Negative (−)
  
**Room-wise Power Distribution:**
```
Main Battery
     │
     ├── Room 1 switch → LED1
     └── Room 2 switch → LED2
```

**Safety in Real Homes:**
Real electricians MCB (Miniature Circuit Breakers) use karte hain
Agar short circuit ho → MCB trip karta hai → fire nahi lagta!

## Practical Lab (45 Minutes)

### Wire Your Smart Home!

### Materials:
- Smart Home model from last session
- Red/Black jumper wires
- 2 LEDs + 2 resistors (220Ω)
- 2 mini switches
- 9V Battery

### Wiring Plan:
```
Battery (+) → Room1 Switch → 220Ω → LED1 (Room 1 Ceiling)
             Room2 Switch → 220Ω → LED2 (Room 2 Ceiling)
All LEDs (−) → Battery (−)
```

### Installation Steps:
1. Small holes cardboard mein LED ke liye punch karo (ceiling)
2. LED ander se push karo — light oopar shine kare
3. Wires walls ke along tape/glue karo (hidden)
4. Switches doors ke paas lagao (like real room switches)
5. Battery room ke bahar rakho

### Test:
Room 1 switch: LED1 ON/OFF?
Room 2 switch: LED2 ON/OFF?
