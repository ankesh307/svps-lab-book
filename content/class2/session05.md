## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Conductors vs Insulators
**Conductor**: Bijli easily flow kar sakti hai
Examples: Copper, Iron, Gold, Salt Water, Human body

**Insulator**: Bijli flow NAHI kar sakti
Examples: Plastic, Rubber, Wood, Glass, Dry Air

```
ATOM LEVEL:
Conductor: Loose electrons → easily move → current flows
Insulator: Tight electrons → can't move → no current
```

**Why wires are copper inside but plastic outside?**
- Copper (inside) = Conductor → bijli carry karta hai
- Plastic (outside) = Insulator → hamare haath safe rehte hain!

**Q2.** Wire copper ka kyun bana hota hai?
