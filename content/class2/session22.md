## Theory (20 Minutes)

### What is Smart Home?
Smart Home = Electronic systems se ghar automate karna

**Smart Home Features:**
- Automatic lights (sensor se)
- Remote-controlled fans aur ACs
- Security alarms
- Smart locks
- Energy monitoring

**Why We're Building a Model:**
Engineers pehle SMALL MODELS banate hain (prototypes) phir real cheez banate hain!
Aaj hum aapka Smart Home prototype banayenge.

**Model Design Planning:**
```
Room 1 (Bedroom):  LED light + switch
Room 2 (Kitchen):  LED light + fan motor
Entrance: Doorbell buzzer
Roof: Exhaust fan
```
