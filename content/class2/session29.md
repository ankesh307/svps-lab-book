## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Why Communication Skills Matter for Engineers
Engineer hona sirf circuit banana nahi hai!
Duniya ke best engineers woh hain jo:
1. Technical problems solve karte hain
2. Unhe EXPLAIN bhi kar sakte hain

**Steve Jobs quote:**
"The most powerful person in the world is the storyteller."

**Engineering Presentation Structure:**
1. **Problem Statement**: Kya problem solve ki?
2. **Solution**: Kya banaya?
3. **How it Works**: Circuit/code explain karo
4. **Demo**: Live dikhaao
5. **What I Learned**: Learning share karo

**Body Language Tips:**
- Eye contact banao
- Slow aur clear bol
- Point mat karo — gesture karo
- Confident posture rakhna

**Presentation Template (3 minutes):**

3. "Yeh kaise kaam karta hai: [EXPLAIN in simple words]"

## Common Problems & Fixes

| Problem | Solution |
|:---|:---|
| Nothing working | Check power connections first |
| Incorrect output | Verify wiring against diagram |
| Code not uploading | Check COM port and board selection |
| Unexpected values | Check sensor connections |

## Quiz (5 Minutes)

**Q1.** Engineer ke liye communication skills kyun zaroori hai?
> Answer: Technical solution banana kaafi nahi — investors, clients, team ko samjhana bhi zaroori hai

**Q2.** Engineering presentation mein kya kya hona chahiye?
> Answer: Problem, Solution, How it works, Demo, Learning

**Q3.** Effective presentation ke liye body language mein kya zaroori hai?
> Answer: Eye contact, slow-clear speech, confident posture, gestures
