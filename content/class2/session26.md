## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Electrolysis and Ionic Conduction
**Pure Water** = Insulator (no free ions)
**Salt Water** = Conductor (NaCl → Na+ and Cl- ions)

**How Saltwater Conducts:**
```
NaCl dissolves → Na+ ions + Cl- ions
Na+ moves to (-) electrode
Cl- moves to (+) electrode
= Current flows!
```

**Electrolysis:**
When current passes through saltwater:
- (+) electrode: Oxygen gas released
- (−) electrode: Hydrogen gas released

**Real World Applications:**
- Chlorine production (water purification)
- Electroplating (gold/silver coating)
- Aluminum smelting
- Hydrogen fuel production!

## Practical Lab (45 Minutes)

### Safety First!
CAUTION: Use ONLY low voltage (9V or less). Never mains electricity near water!

### Materials:
- 9V Battery
- LED + 220Ω Resistor
- Two pencils (sharpened both ends)
- Glass of water
- Salt
- Alligator clip wires

### Experiment Steps:

**Test 1 — Pure Water:**
```
Pencil A (+ probe) → water → Pencil B (− probe) → LED circuit
Pure water: LED stays OFF (insulator)
```

**Test 2 — Salt Water:**
Add 2-3 teaspoons of salt. Stir well.
```
Same setup: LED may glow DIM (conductor!)
```

**Test 3 — More Salt:**
Add more salt → Does LED get brighter?

**Observe:** Bubbles on pencil tips! That's oxygen and hydrogen gas!
This is ELECTROLYSIS happening live!
