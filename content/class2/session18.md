## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Engineering a Switch from Scratch
Real switches mein yahi principle kaam karta hai:
1. Ek conducting part dusre se physically TOUCH ho
2. Connection complete → current flows

**Simple Switch Components:**
- **Two conductors** (metal strips/paperclips)
- **Insulating base** (cardboard/wood)
- **Mechanism** to bring them together (spring/lever)

**DIY Switch Types we can make:**
1. Paperclip switch (lever type)
2. Coin switch (touch type)
3. Foil switch (pressure type)
4. Tilt switch (mercury/ball type)

**Real Switches Inside:**
If you carefully open an old non-working switch, you'll see the same principle — two metal contacts coming together!

## Practical Lab (45 Minutes)

### Make Your Own Switch!

### Version 1 — Paperclip Lever Switch:
**Materials:** Cardboard, 2 paperclips, brass fasteners, wires

```
Cardboard base:
[Paperclip1] ─── brass fastener ─── [Wire to +]
     |
     | (bends down to touch)
     ↓
[Paperclip2] ─── brass fastener ─── [Wire to LED]
```
Press top paperclip → Touches bottom → Circuit complete!

### Version 2 — Foil Touch Switch:
- Fold foil → normal position = open = no contact
- Press = foil pieces touch = circuit closed

### Test Circuit:
```
Battery(+) → YOUR SWITCH → LED+Resistor → Battery(−)
```

### Challenge: Can you make a switch that:
- Stays ON when pressed (latch)?
- Turns ON only when TWO buttons pressed together (AND gate)?
