## Theory (20 Minutes)

### How Electric Motors Work
Motor ek device hai jo **electrical energy** ko **mechanical (rotational) energy** mein convert karta hai.

**Principle:** Magnetic fields interact karte hain → motor shaft rotate hoti hai

```
Electric Current → Magnetic Field → Shaft Rotation → Propeller Spins → AIR FLOWS!
```

**Parts of a Motor:**
1. **Stator**: Fixed magnetic field part
2. **Rotor/Armature**: Rotating part
3. **Shaft**: Rotation output
4. **Brushes**: Current connection

**RPM (Revolutions Per Minute)**: Motor kitni baar ek minute mein ghoomta hai
Normal toy motor = 3000-6000 RPM at 9V

## Practical Lab (45 Minutes)

### Components:
- 9V Battery + Snap
- Toy DC Motor
- Propeller (plastic fan blades)
- Slide Switch
- Jumper Wires
- Cardboard base

### Assembly Steps:
1. Propeller ko motor shaft par push karke fit karo
2. Wiring connect karo:
```
Battery(+) → Switch → Motor(Red wire)
Motor(Black wire) → Battery(−)
```
3. Switch ON karo → Motor spins → Air flows!

### Safety Rules:
- Fingers propeller ke PAAS mat rakho!
- Agar motor zyada garam ho jaaye → immediately disconnect

### Observe:
- Motor kitni fast spin kar raha hai?
- Paas rakho — kya hawa aa rahi hai?
- Battery voltage km karo — speed badali?
