## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Gear Motor vs Toy Motor
| Property | Toy Motor | Gear Motor |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Very FAST (6000 RPM) | Slow (60-300 RPM) |
| Torque (strength) | Weak | STRONG |
| Can carry weight? | No | Yes! |
| Use case | Fan | Robot car wheels |

### What are Gears?
Gears ek doosre se mesh karke rotation **speed ko kam karke torque (force) badhaate** hain!

```
Input: 6000 RPM (fast, weak)
Gear Box reduces speed
Output: 200 RPM (slow, STRONG)
```

**Key Principle**: Speed aur Torque trade-off karte hain!
Speed badhao → Torque ghata
Torque badhao → Speed ghata

## Practical Lab (45 Minutes)

### Components:
- 9V Battery + Snap
- Gear Motor (BO Motor) x2
- Rubber Wheels x2
- Cardboard chassis / acrylic plate
- Caster wheel (support)
- Jumper Wires

### Assembly:
1. Gear motors chassis ke left-right mein lagao
2. Wheels motor shaft par push karo
3. Caster wheel peeche lagao (balance ke liye)
4. Battery connect karo:
```
Battery(+) → Both motors (Red)
Both motors (Black) → Battery(−)
```

### Strength Test!
- Car floor par chalao — kya smoothly jata hai?
- Hath se rokne ki koshish karo — kitna strong hai?
- Compare karo toy motor vs gear motor strength!

## Quiz (5 Minutes)

**Q1.** Gear motor aur toy motor mein kya fark hai?
> Answer: Gear motor slower hai par much stronger (more torque), toy motor fast but weak

**Q2.** Gear box kya karta hai?
> Answer: Speed ko reduce karta hai aur torque increase karta hai

**Q3.** Robot car mein gear motor kyun use hota hai?
> Answer: Car ka weight carry karne ke liye zyada torque chahiye
