## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Speed vs Torque — The Engineering Tradeoff

**Torque** = Rotational force (strength)
**RPM** = Rotational speed

```
High Torque + Low Speed = Gear Motor (Robot car, Elevator)
Low Torque + High Speed = Toy Motor (Fan, Drill at no load)
```

**Power Formula:**
Power (Watts) = Voltage × Current
Both motors at 9V use similar power, but gear motor uses it DIFFERENTLY.

**Analogy:**
- Bicycle 1st gear = Low speed, easy to pedal uphill (high torque)
- Bicycle top gear = High speed on flat road (low torque)
Same legs (power), different output!

### Comparison Experiment:

**Test 1 — Speed Race:**

- Which spins faster? ___
- Measure approximate RPM by counting rotations in 10 seconds × 6

**Test 2 — Strength Test (Torque):**
Try to STOP each motor with your fingers:
- Toy motor: Easy to stop? ___
- Gear motor: Hard to stop? ___

**Test 3 — Load Carrying:**
Place a small weight (eraser) on a wheel:
- Toy motor: Can it still spin? ___
- Gear motor: Can it carry the weight? ___
