## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Newton's 3rd Law: Action-Reaction
"Har action ka ek equal aur opposite reaction hota hai"

**Fan Thrust Concept:**
```
Motor → Propeller spins → Air pushed BACKWARD
                          ↓ (reaction)
                    Car moves FORWARD!
```

**Real-World Examples:**
- ✈️ Airplane: Jet engines push air backward → Plane goes forward
- 🚀 Rocket: Burning gas ejected downward → Rocket goes UP
- ⛵ Hovercraft: Air blown down → Hovers up

**Why Do Wheels Help?**
Without wheels: Friction too high → Car doesn't move
With wheels: Low rolling friction → Air thrust is enough to move!

## Practical Lab (45 Minutes)

### Build Air Thrust Car!

### Materials:
- Cardboard base (chassis)
- 4 small wheels (or bottle caps with toothpick axle)
- Toy motor + Propeller
- 9V Battery + Snap
- Switch
- Jumper Wires
- Hot glue / tape

### Assembly:
1. Cardboard chassis cut karo (20cm × 10cm)
2. Axles: Toothpick/straw ke through chassis holes mein, wheels attach
3. Motor chassis ke BACK par mount karo (propeller facing BACKWARD)
4. Battery front par rakho (balance ke liye)
5. Switch connect karo

```
[Battery][Switch] ─── [Motor+Propeller →→ Air →→]
     Front                    Rear
                              Air pushes backward
                              Car moves FORWARD!
```

### Experiment:
- On smooth floor: Does it move?
- On rough carpet: Does it move?
- Add more battery: Faster?

## Quiz (5 Minutes)

**Q1.** Air thrust car kaun si physics law use karta hai?
> Answer: Newton's 3rd Law — action-reaction, air backward → car forward

**Q2.** Airplane thrust aur air thrust car mein kya similarity hai?
> Answer: Dono air backward push karte hain aur forward momentum lete hain — same principle!

**Q3.** Car mein wheels kyun zaroori hain?
> Answer: Friction reduce karne ke liye — bina wheels ke air thrust kaafi nahi hogi
