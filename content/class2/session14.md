## Theory (20 Minutes)

### Steering a Vibration Robot
Weight position change karo → Movement direction change hoti hai!

```
Weight center mein: Straight ahead
Weight LEFT side par: Turns RIGHT
Weight RIGHT side par: Turns LEFT
```

**Physics**: Vibration energy unequally distribute hoti hai → higher energy side se dusri taraf push hota hai

**Compare to Real Robots:**
- Real robots mein steering = different speed on left/right wheels
- Bristlebot mein steering = weight distribution change
- **Same concept, different method!**

**Design Thinking:**
Engineers jab robot banate hain to pehle simple prototypes banate hain (jaise bristlebot) phir complex systems design karte hain!

### Materials:
- Your Bristlebot from last session

### Steering Experiments:

**Experiment 1 — Straight:**
Weight center mein → Bristlebot straight chalta hai

**Experiment 2 — Left Turn:**
Small clay piece LEFT side mein tape karo
→ Does it turn right? ___

**Experiment 3 — Right Turn:**
Move clay to RIGHT side
→ Does it turn left? ___

**Experiment 4 — Tight Circle:**
Put weight at extreme corner
→ What happens? ___

### Design Challenge:

1. Go in a straight line for 30 cm?
