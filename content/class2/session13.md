## Theory (20 Minutes)

### What is a Bristlebot?
Bristlebot = Brush + Vibration Bot! 
Dunya ka simplest robot — sirf ek motor aur ek brush!

**How it Moves:**
Vibration motor vibrate karta hai → Brush ke bristles (taale/kante) angled hote hain →
Vibration + Angled bristles = NET FORWARD MOVEMENT!

```
Bristle angle:  \\\\ (angled backward)
Vibration:      ↑↓↑↓ (up and down)
Net movement:   → → → (forward!)
```

**Physics Concept**: Anisotropic friction!
Bristles ek direction mein zyada resist karte hain dusre se — yehi movement create karta hai!

**Real Robots using same principle:**
- Vibrating seed planters in agriculture
- Micro-robots in medical research

## Practical Lab (45 Minutes)

### Build Your Bristlebot!

### Materials:
- Old toothbrush head (cut off the handle)
- Coin vibration motor
- CR2032 button cell battery OR 9V with wires
- Double-sided tape OR hot glue

### Assembly:
1. Toothbrush ka handle cut karo — sirf bristle part rakhna hai
2. Coin motor ko bristle side ke UPAR tape/glue se chipkao
3. Battery ko motor ke RED wire se connect karo (+)
4. Black wire ko battery (−) se connect karo
5. Smooth floor par rakho aur observe karo!

### Expected Result:
Bristlebot SLOWLY forward move karta hai! 🐛

### Troubleshooting:
- Nahi chal raha? Battery check karo
- Circles mein chal raha? Weight distribution uneven hai — adjust karo

## Quiz (5 Minutes)

**Q1.** Bristlebot kaise move karta hai?
> Answer: Vibration + angled bristles = net forward movement through anisotropic friction

**Q2.** Bristlebot banane ke liye minimum kya chahiye?
> Answer: Ek vibration motor, ek battery, aur ek toothbrush bristle part

**Q3.** Agar bristlebot circles mein chale to problem kya hai?
> Answer: Weight distribution uneven hai — motor center mein nahi hai
